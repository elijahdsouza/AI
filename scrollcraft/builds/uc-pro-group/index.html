Be honest with yourself

Who we’re for. Who we’re not.

We are choosy on purpose. The rooms only stay valuable if the people in them are the right ones. Read both columns before you join.

This is for you if you are

  • Actively building a business, not still deciding whether to start one
  • Past the program stage and feeling the momentum quietly drain away
  • Willing to show up in person. The dinners and rooms are where this compounds
  • Generous by default. You give intros and advice before you ask for them
  • Ready to be coachable, to hear hard feedback and actually act on it
  • Playing a long game. You want peers for the next decade, not a quick win

It is probably not for you if you

  • Want a guaranteed investor cheque. We open doors, we do not sell outcomes
  • Are looking for a cheap coworking desk
  • Are idea-stage with nothing underway yet
  • Want a program with an end date and a certificate
  • Plan to sell to the room rather than build in it. No grifters

Entry is by fit, not by wallet.

A business underway, real traction for your stage, and a growth trajectory. We would rather turn away revenue than put the wrong person at the table. If now is not your moment, come to a dinner and come back when it is.

Before you decide

Everything else you might be weighing up.

There are no paid members yet. Why join now?

Because that is exactly what you are buying. The Pro Group opens with its founding intake, and the first 50 people set the standard at the door, the rhythm of the month and the culture of the room. The wider Uncommon Collective community is 2,000 people and six years old, so the room is not being built from nothing. It is being built from a community that already exists, with you helping to choose who is in it. If you would rather join something finished, join later and pay the later rate.

Am I the right stage? I run a small business, not a startup.

Yes. This is explicitly not a tech-only room. Service businesses with real revenue and a small team, emerging small businesses on a growth trajectory, high-growth startups, operators and creators building something of their own. What matters is that something is underway and you are going somewhere with it, not what category you fit.

What does the founding rate actually mean?

The prices on this page are guaranteed for the first 50 members and close on 3 October 2026. Join in that window and your rate stays locked for 12 months, even as prices rise behind you.

What is the time commitment?

Realistically, one evening and one working day a month gets you most of the value. Everything is optional and nothing is graded. The people who get the most out of it show up in person, because that is where relationships actually form.

What if I cannot make the events?

The month repeats, so missing one is not missing your chance. Coworking days, dinners and walks all recur, and the perks, tools and growth services run whether you attend or not. If you can never make anything in person, this is honestly not the right spend for you.

How is this different from coworking or a program?

Coworking sells you proximity and calls it community. A program sells you a plan with an end date. We are the third space that picks up where both stop: it grows as your business does, it is built on relationships rather than a curriculum, and it has no finish line to fall off.

Can I cancel or change tiers later?

Yes. No lock-in contracts, cancel anytime, and move up or down a tier whenever your needs change. There is also a 30 day money-back guarantee.
